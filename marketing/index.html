What it is

A home for throwaway and team-sized tools: one-pagers, workflows, prototypes. Built in Claude, Cursor, or ChatGPT — then put on the internet in a minute.

Not Lovable

We don’t generate your app. We deploy and share what you (or your agent) already built — closer to “paste → live link” than another builder.

Already on AWS?

The open-source aft CLI deploys static sites and Next.js into your account — Vercel-like DX, AWS ownership. This page was deployed with it.

Why aft?

As in after Vercel — and the aft of a ship: your infra, your cloud. Short enough to own, punchy as a CLI: aft deploy.
