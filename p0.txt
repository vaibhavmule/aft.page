POSITIONING (say this)
===============================================================================

AFT is the publishing and permission layer for software created by AI agents.
Your agent made it. aft makes it live, persistent, and shareable — like a Google Doc.

Not “cheaper Vercel.” Not “another Drop.”
Job: agent output → durable URL → who can open it.
Moat vs Drop/Vercel: invite ACL now; IP whitelist / perimeter at $499 when they
point at Deployment Protection.

LOI counts as Phase 4 proof. Details: docs/PRICING.md · docs/COMPETITION.md
