Cookie Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026 · Last updated: 9 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how aft.page ("aft", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites work, improve security and remember that you are signed in. Cookies may be session cookies, which expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain for a defined period unless deleted earlier.

2. How aft.page uses cookies

aft.page uses strictly necessary cookies for authentication. They are required to sign you in and keep you signed in.

Cookie Purpose Type Duration
aft_session Keeps an authenticated user signed in to aft.page. First-party, persistent (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) Up to 30 days
aft_oauth CSRF protection during Sign in with Google. Set only for the Google redirect. First-party, session-short (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) Up to 10 minutes

3. No analytics or advertising cookies

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not embed third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the aft.page website or dashboard.

4. Cookie-less audience measurement

aft.page measures deployment and page-view activity server-side without writing any cookie or unique identifier to the visitor's device. To approximate unique deployers, we use a truncated one-way hash of the IP address; we do not store raw IP addresses in these metrics, and the value cannot be reversed to identify an individual visitor.

5. Consent

The strictly necessary cookies above do not require consent in the same way as optional cookies, because they are needed to provide core service functions such as secure login. Where we introduce any non-essential cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will request consent where required by applicable law.

6. How to manage cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including by deleting existing cookies or blocking future cookies. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of aft.page, such as staying signed in, from functioning correctly.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the date above.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, contact hello@aft.page.
