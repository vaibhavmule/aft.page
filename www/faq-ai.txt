# Frequently Asked Questions - aft.page

Lang: en
Website: [https://aft.page](https://aft.page)
Last Updated: 2026-08-09

[About the Product]

Q: What is aft.page?
A: aft.page turns software your AI agent builds into a live, shareable HTTPS URL
   in seconds — no repository, cloud account, or build setup. Deploy by dropping
   files, pasting HTML, or letting your agent call the MCP.
URL: [aft.page](https://aft.page/)

Q: What does the name aft.page mean?
A: The name comes from the aft of a ship (the back of the vessel): take what you
   built and put it out into the world.

Q: What category is aft.page in?
A: A cloud for small software — the publishing and permission layer for software
   created by AI agents, not a website builder or chat product.

[Getting Started]

Q: Do I need an account to deploy?
A: No. Anonymous deploy gives you a live URL immediately. You only
   sign in when you want to claim a page, update it, make it private, or invite
   people.
URL: [Drop files](https://aft.page/drop/)

Q: Is aft.page free?
A: Yes — free to go live during beta (claim, private invite, secrets).
   Personal is 30 days free, then $19/month. Team and Enterprise are one contact.
   Billing has not started; waitlist members hear before anything is charged.
URL: [Pricing](https://aft.page/#pricing)

Q: What can I deploy, and what are the limits?
A: Single HTML documents or multi-file static sites and SPAs (include index.html).
   Limits: 500 files, 25 MB per file, and 100 MB total.

[Agents and MCP]

Q: How do AI agents deploy to aft.page?
A: Any agent that speaks MCP can use the remote server at
   https://mcp.aft.page/mcp with deploy (plus aft_deploys and aft_rollback),
   or call the API directly. It works with Claude, Cursor, Codex, and ChatGPT.
   Humans can also use Drop, curl, or the hosted CLI:
   `curl -fsSL https://aft.page/install | sh` then `aft deploy`
   (no login for deploy; after claim use `aft login` for rename, env, rollback).
URL: [MCP docs](https://aft.page/mcp) · [Docs](https://aft.page/docs) · [Agent index](https://aft.page/docs.md)

Q: Which MCP tools does aft.page expose?
A: A thin surface: deploy, aft_deploys, aft_rollback, and aft_health. First
   deploy mints a slug + editToken; later deploys with that token keep the same
   URL. MCP is a deploy adapter, not a control plane.
URL: [Agent index](https://aft.page/llms.txt)

[Sharing and Ownership]

Q: Can I make a page private or invite people?
A: Yes. Claim a deploy, then keep it public, make it private, or invite specific
   people by email — and revoke access anytime.
URL: [Preview / claim](https://aft.page/preview)

Q: Can I use my own domain?
A: Yes after you claim. Custom domains are invite-only during beta: project
   Domains tab → request access → CNAME the hostname to cname.aft.page. HTTPS
   issues from there. Your default URL stays.
URL: [Docs](https://aft.page/docs/domains/)

Q: How is aft.page different from Vercel, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages?
A: Those are built for Big Software: repositories, builds, and cloud accounts.
   aft is the publishing and permission layer for small software that one person
   or a small team uses — an instant URL, invite-by-email sharing, clear
   ownership, and secrets, without the ceremony.
URL: [Compare](https://aft.page/vs/vercel/)
