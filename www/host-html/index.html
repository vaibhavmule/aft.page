    FAQ

    Is HTML hosting really free?

    Yes for anonymous static sites within the published limits. We don’t put ads on your pages. Unclaimed sites are deleted after 30 days unused — claim to keep them.

    Is this like a website builder?

    No. You bring the HTML (often from an AI agent). aft.page only makes it live and shareable.

    Custom domains?

    Yes after you claim — invite-only during beta. Project → Domains, request access, then CNAME to cname.aft.page. You keep the default URL too.

    Prefer agents over paste? aft.page is agent-first on the home page — or connect MCP once and skip the form.
