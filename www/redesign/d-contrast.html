FAQ

Questions, answered

What aft.page is, who it's for, and how it works — in plain language.

What is aft.page?

aft is a cloud for small software — a live link you can share. Drop your files or let your agent deploy. No account.

Do I need an account to deploy?

No. Anonymous deploy gives you a live shareable URL immediately. You only sign in when you want to claim the app, update it, make it private, or invite people.

Is aft.page free?

Yes — going live is free during beta, and claiming your URL to keep it is free too. Private apps, invite-by-email sharing, and per-site secrets are part of Personal ($19/mo after 30 days free). Team and Enterprise are one conversation. We will email the waitlist before charging anyone.

How is aft.page different from Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or Vercel?

Deploy a static site: aft.page, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or Vercel. aft.page — no account, drop dist/ or MCP, live HTTPS URL in seconds. Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, and Vercel — better when you already have a git repo, CI, preview PRs, or a big production setup.
