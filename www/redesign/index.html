Five ways the aft homepage could look

E is the full rewrite — new words, new structure, new layout, no recycled homepage copy. D is its layout sibling (same re-structure, old copy retained). A-C are visual-world experiments. The shipped homepage is untouched — open each, then pick.

E — Rewrite · recommended

Recopied, restructured, relaid

Every line re-authored, every section re-thought. Hero: "Go live like you share a Doc." Two-way race card (the usual way ≈ 40 min vs aft ≈ 2 s). Share as "Hand it out," keep as "The link outlives the hype," compare as "The ceremony isn't a feature," pricing as "Free is the whole free tier." No recycled homepage copy.

Drafting sheet

Blueprint register on a cool paper-void. Dimension rules, DWG title block, corner ticks, marginalia, section numbers (SPEC-02 / CMP-04). Blueprint blue is used as the drawing-annotation color, not a brand hue.

C — Index

Numbered editorial

A persistent index rail on the left, giant outline numerals per section, stroke-outline hero type, pill CTAs, magazine rules. Green stays the only live/success voice. The page is a table of contents you scroll through.
