When Vercel is the right call

  • Production frontend apps and framework hosting (Next.js, etc.)
  • CI/CD from a Git repository with preview deployments
  • A team shipping and maintaining one product over time
  • Edge functions and serverless at scale

When aft.page is the right call

  • Your agent just built something and you want a live URL now
  • No repo, no build, no cloud account to wire up
  • Share privately with a teammate — invite by email, revoke anytime
  • Your own domain after claim (request access, CNAME to cname.aft.page)
  • Internal tools, dashboards, prototypes you update in place and stop when done

Bottom line: Vercel is built for Big Software. aft.page is the publishing and permission layer for the small software agents make — an instant URL, private sharing, and clear ownership without the ceremony.

Common questions

Is aft.page a Vercel alternative?

For small, agent-made software, yes — aft.page gives you an instant URL and private sharing without repositories or builds. For large production framework apps with CI/CD, Vercel is the better fit. They do different jobs.

Can I deploy without connecting a Git repository?

Yes. With aft.page you drop files, paste HTML, or have your agent call the MCP. There is no repository to connect and no build step to configure.

Does aft.page support private sharing?

Yes. Claim a page, make it private, and invite specific people by email — and revoke access anytime.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes after you claim. Request access on the project Domains tab, then CNAME to cname.aft.page. HTTPS issues from there. The *.aft.page URL stays.
